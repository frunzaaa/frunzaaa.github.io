Privacy Policy

— Nyxbo (com.nyxbo)

Nyxbo is a pixel-art editor that runs entirely on your device. It has no accounts, no ads, and no internet connection. Necaz Software has no servers and never receives any of your data. This page explains exactly what the app does and does not do.

Last updated: August 7, 2026

Who we are

Nyxbo ("the app") is developed and published by Necaz Software. If you have any questions about this policy or your privacy, contact us at necaz.software@gmail.com.

Data we collect

None. Nyxbo does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and makes no network connections at all.

Your artwork stays on your device

Everything you create in Nyxbo — drawings, layers, frames, palettes, imported reference images, and app settings — is stored locally on your device.

  • Your artwork never leaves your device through the app and is never uploaded to us or anyone else.
  • We have no accounts and no servers. We cannot see, access, or recover your work.
  • Exported images are written to a Nyxbo folder on your device that you control.

Permissions the app uses

  • Storage — used only so you can import your own images to draw over, and so exported images are saved to a folder on your device. Your files never leave your device, and the app does not read anything you don't explicitly open or export.

What we do not collect

  • No accounts, names, emails, or passwords.
  • No location data.
  • No contacts or photos beyond an image you explicitly choose to import.
  • No analytics, advertising, or usage tracking of any kind.
  • No internet connection — the app works fully offline.

Children's privacy

Nyxbo is a general-audience creative tool, is safe for all ages, and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your privacy? Email necaz.software@gmail.com.
