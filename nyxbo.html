Nyxbo

— pixel-art studio. draw, animate, export. fully offline.

Nyxbo is a fast, focused pixel-art editor that fits in your pocket. Draw sprites, build animations frame by frame, and export game-ready assets — all on your device, with no accounts, no ads, and no internet.

Built with Flutter, it runs smoothly on phones and tablets. Real RGB palettes for every bit depth (2 to 8-bit / up to 256 colors), layers, onion-skinned animation, image import for tracing, and export to spritesheets, strips, separate frames, or animated GIF.

Current features

Pencil, eraser, bucket fill, line and rectangle tools, eyedropper, and a freehand tool. Real RGB palettes for every bit depth from 2 to 8-bit (up to 256 colors) with fully undoable format switching. Layers with opacity, visibility, reordering and duplication. Frame-by-frame animation with onion-skinning and adjustable FPS. Import any image or spritesheet and trace over it. Two-finger pan & zoom, move-whole-image, and fit-to-screen. Export as spritesheet PNG + JSON atlas, horizontal/vertical strips, separate frames, upscaled PNG, or animated GIF. Unlimited undo/redo. All offline — no internet, no tracking, no cloud.
